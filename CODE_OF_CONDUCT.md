# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free project community.

Be constructive, assume good intent, focus criticism on technical work, and protect confidential or
client-supplied information. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and publication of another person's
private information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior conflicts
with these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner.
